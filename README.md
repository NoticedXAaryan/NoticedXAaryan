### 📖 About Me

I'm a technology enthusiast driven by a lifelong curiosity for how things work. My journey began in second grade with an old office PC and evolved into building and troubleshooting computers by the fifth grade. My programming adventure started with HTML in sixth grade, and by tenth, I was proficient in C++, JavaScript, and Python, while running a part-time passion for custom PC builds and microcontroller projects.

In eleventh grade, I discovered the power of data science and found my calling. This led me to pursue a PG Certification in Data Science alongside my formal education. Today, I channel this passion into two main areas: my academic pursuit of a BCA (Hons) degree and my entrepreneurial venture, **Blendable3D**, where I get to blend imagination with precision every day.
