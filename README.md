## 👋 About Me

I'm a technology enthusiast who started on an old office PC in second grade and never looked back. By fifth grade I was building and troubleshooting computers. By tenth grade I had a grip on HTML, CSS, C++, JavaScript, and Python — and a growing obsession with microcontrollers and electronics.

In eleventh grade, data science found me. I dove into a PG certification in Data Science, ML & Neural Networks, and haven't stopped since. Today I channel that energy into my BCA degree, my startup **Blendable3D** (3D printing & rapid prototyping), and deep dives into RNNs, LSTMs, and Reinforcement Learning.

> *Curious by nature. Builder by choice.*
